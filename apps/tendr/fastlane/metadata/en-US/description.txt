Tendr is a calm, private journal for people on a regular medication schedule. Record each dose, keep track of your weight and see how things are going, all on your iPhone.

LOG IN SECONDS
• Record a dose in mg with the time and an optional note
• Log weigh-ins yourself, or read your body weight from Apple Health
• Edit or remove any entry later

STAY ON SCHEDULE
• Choose weekly dose days, a repeating interval or hand-picked dates
• Get a gentle reminder on your dose days
• See your next dose at a glance on the home screen

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Weight trend with a 7-day average
• Set a goal weight and see how far you have come
• Weekly and total change for the period you choose

YOUR DOSES IN CONTEXT
• A chart of your logged doses over time, with a simple estimated level based on a half-life model
• Clearly labelled as an estimate: it is not a measured level and never suggests a dose

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in and no servers
• Your journal is stored only on your iPhone
• Apple Health access is read-only and limited to body weight
• No ads, analytics or tracking

A JOURNAL, NOT MEDICAL ADVICE
Tendr helps you keep a record. It does not tell you what to take, how much to take or when to change a dose, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always follow the instructions of your prescriber or healthcare professional.

Folding is a personal fold tracker for Duo. Set up your open and close automations in Shortcuts, test them in the app, and start seeing the patterns in your day.

• Track folds, time open, and estimated travel.
• Explore daily, weekly, monthly, and all-time statistics.
• Keep private history in sync with iCloud.
• Choose your colours, favourite stats, and widgets.
• Join optional weekly, monthly, and all-time leaderboards.
• Export your history as JSON or CSV, or reset it whenever you want.

All features are free. Optional tips unlock nothing and give no leaderboard advantage. Travel is an estimate based on recorded events, not a measurement of hinge wear.

Privacy policy

Your journal stays on your iPhone.

Tendr is a dose and weight journal with no account and no servers. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information.

What Tendr stores

Everything you enter (doses, vials, weights, notes, your schedule and your goal) is saved in a single file inside Tendr's own storage on your iPhone. Tendr has no server, so this information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

We, the developer, cannot see, access or recover your journal.

No account, no tracking

  • No sign-up, login or account of any kind.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data sold or shared with third parties.

Tendr's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Tendr asks for read-only access to your body weight, and nothing else. Tendr never writes to Apple Health.

Weights read from Apple Health are copied into your journal on your iPhone so your progress stays current, and Tendr may refresh them in the background when Health records a new weight. They are used only to show your weight and progress inside the app.

Health data is never shared with anyone, never sent off your device by Tendr, and never used for advertising, marketing or data mining.

You can turn off access at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Tendr.

Notifications

Dose reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone from the schedule you set. No push service is involved and nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your schedule removes any pending reminders.

Backups

Tendr does not upload or sync your journal itself. Like other app data, your journal is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup to your computer) according to your device settings, and is restored with them. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Tendr does not currently offer a separate export or file backup.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual doses and weights in the Journal tab. Deleting Tendr from your iPhone permanently removes its journal from the device. Weights that came from Apple Health stay in the Health app, where you manage them.

Copies may remain in older iPhone backups until those backups are replaced or deleted.

Children

Tendr is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

Tendr is a journal, not a dosing guide. Medication graphs are simplified estimates, not measured levels. Always follow the instructions of your prescriber and talk to your clinician about your treatment.

Changes to this policy

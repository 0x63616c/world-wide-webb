Frequently asked

How do I log a dose?

On the Home tab, tap Log dose. Enter the amount you took, check the date and time, add a note if you like, then save.

To fix a past entry, open the Journal tab and tap it to edit.

How does Apple Health sync work?

In the Settings tab, tap Connect Apple Health and allow access to body weight. Tendr reads weight only and never writes to Health.

After that, new weights from Health appear automatically, and you can tap Sync body weight any time to refresh. To stop, turn off access in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Tendr.

How do I back up or move my data to a new iPhone?

Your journal lives only on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone backups. Keep iCloud Backup (or a computer backup) turned on, and when you set up a new iPhone, restore from that backup or use Quick Start to transfer. Tendr and its journal come across with it.

Tendr has no account or cloud sync of its own, so there's nothing to sign in to.

How do I delete everything?

To remove single entries, swipe left on a dose or weight in the Journal tab and tap Delete. To clear reminders, clear your schedule.

To erase everything, delete the Tendr app from your iPhone. Its journal is removed with it. Weights imported from Apple Health remain in the Health app.

Why doesn't the medication level match a lab result?

The level graph is a simplified estimate based on your logged doses. It isn't a measured blood level and shouldn't be used to decide doses.

Medical note

Tendr is a personal journal, not medical advice or a dosing guide. Follow your prescriber's instructions and talk to your clinician about any questions about your treatment. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
